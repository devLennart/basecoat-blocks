Can I try Acme before paying?
Yes. Every workspace starts with a 14-day trial of the Studio plan, with no card required. When the trial ends you can pick a plan or drop to the free Solo tier.
How do you handle my data?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the region you choose, and never used to train models. You can export or delete everything at any time.
Can we move over from another tool?
Importers bring over projects, tasks, comments, and attachments from the most common tools. Larger teams get a guided migration on the Scale plan.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits and education?
Registered non-profits and schools get 50% off any paid plan. Contact us with proof of status and we'll apply it to your workspace.
What happens if we cancel?
You keep access until the end of the billing period. After that the workspace becomes read-only for 30 days so you can export your work.

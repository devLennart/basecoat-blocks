Pricing questions

Is there a free plan?
Yes. The Free plan includes three projects and 100 assistant messages a month, with no card required.
How do upgrades work?
Upgrade from Settings at any time. New limits apply right away and we prorate the first invoice.
Can I switch plans mid-cycle?
Yes. Moving up is prorated today. Moving down takes effect at the next renewal, so you keep what you paid for.
Do you offer annual billing?
Annual plans save 20% and are billed once a year by card or invoice.
What happens if I cancel?
Your workspace stays read-only for 30 days, so you can export everything.

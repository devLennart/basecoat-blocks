Acme Agents are here

Today we're launching Acme Agents, a way to hand repetitive work to an assistant that runs on its own and asks before it does anything risky.

Agents are basically like little helpers that you can set up to do a lot of different things for you automatically in the background, and they will also check with you first before they do something that might be dangerous.
